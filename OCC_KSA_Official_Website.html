Self-Help & Dignity in the Kingdom.

Overseas Chitrali's Community (OCC) is a self-help based welfare organization uniting the Chitrali diaspora across Saudi Arabia — from Riyadh to Jeddah, Dammam to Abha and beyond. Through our structured funds (Death, Operation, Injury, Deportation & Emergency Relief) and a city-wise coordinator network, no Chitrali brother faces hardship alone.

Who We Are

About Our Community

Founded in May 2023, Overseas Chitrali's Community (OCC) was established by Chitrali expatriates in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia with a single resolve: every Chitrali in KSA is our responsibility. Within the first year, 476 members from more than 20 cities registered — workers, drivers, technicians, engineers, doctors and businessmen — all contributing to one shared welfare system.

OCC is run on a "Self Helping Based" model: every member contributes small, fixed amounts to dedicated funds. When a brother is deported, hospitalized, operated on, injured at work, passes away, or exits the Kingdom, the community responds immediately — financially and physically — through its city coordinators.

Governed by an elected Cabinet, Shura (Advisory Council) and Controller, with accounts reported to every member, OCC is non-political, non-profit, and fully voluntary.

Our Mission

To protect every Chitrali member of the community in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — in life, in illness, in deportation, and in death — through a transparent, member-funded, self-help welfare system.

🔭

Our Vision

A united, self-reliant Chitrali diaspora in the Kingdom, where no family — in KSA or back home in Chitral — is left without support in its hour of need.

🤝

Our Values

Unity (Ittihad) • Sincerity (Ikhlas) • Transparency (Shafafiyat) • Volunteerism • Full respect for the laws and hospitality of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its people.

What We Do

Objectives of the Community

  • Provide immediate financial support from the Death Fund to the family of any deceased member, and arrange dignified repatriation to Chitral.
  • Support members undergoing surgery through the Operation Fund, and those injured at work through the Injury Fund.
  • Assist deported members with relief payments and help re-establish their livelihoods.
  • Provide Final Exit relief to members leaving the Kingdom permanently.
  • Run an Emergency Relief Fund for sudden crises — accidents, detention, hospitalization — and aid un-registered Chitralis in genuine need.
  • Raise appeal-based donations (Helping by Appeal) across all member groups for major emergencies beyond fund limits.
  • Guide new arrivals on Iqama, employment, banking and life in the Kingdom, and connect them with their city coordinator.
  • Organize condolence visits (Ta'ziyat) and congratulations (Mubarakbad) for member families across KSA.
  • Collect monthly/yearly contributions transparently through city coordinators and publish audited accounts to all members.
  • Maintain unity of all Chitrali groups and cooperate with the Embassy of Pakistan and Saudi authorities whenever required.
Our Safety Net

Welfare Funds & Relief Programs

🕌

Death Fund — 100 SR

Every member contributes 100 SAR to the Death Fund. In the event of a member's death, the fund provides immediate support to the grieving family and coordinates full repatriation of the deceased to Chitral — documentation, ticketing and escort.

🏥

Operation Fund — 15 SR

A dedicated fund for members undergoing surgery or major medical procedures, ensuring no brother delays an operation due to money.

🩹

Injury Fund — 15 SR

Immediate relief for members injured at work or in accidents — covering initial medical costs until other support arrives.

✈️

Deported Members Fund — 5 SR

Supports members who face deportation with relief payments and reintegration assistance for them and their families back home.

🚪

Final Exit Relief

Financial assistance for members leaving the Kingdom permanently — because we take care of our brothers until the last airport goodbye.

🚨

Emergency Relief & Appeals

For crises beyond the funds — accidents, detention, disasters — the community launches appeal-based collections across all member groups. In 2023 alone, SAR 58,840 was distributed in emergency relief and SAR 58,550 through appeals.

Join the Community

Membership is open to every Chitrali legally residing in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Registration numbers are issued sequentially (A01, A02… / B477…).

  1. Contact the coordinator of your city (see the Coordinators section).
  2. Submit your registration with photograph and basic details.
  3. Pay the one-time registration fee and fund contributions.
  4. Receive your OCC ID Card and be added to your city group.
One-Time Registration: SAR 50  |  OCC ID Card: SAR 10
Yearly Payment: SAR 100 (payable in monthly installments of SAR 50/100 via your coordinator)
Welfare Funds: Death Fund SAR 100 • Operation SAR 15 • Injury SAR 15 • Deport Fund SAR 5

📱 Members join their city WhatsApp group after registration, coordinated monthly by the city coordinator.
